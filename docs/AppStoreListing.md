**Description:**
Truefit turns your iPhone into a scan-to-print workbench.

SCAN
Walk around an object with the guided LiDAR scanner and Truefit builds a 3D model right on your phone. Already scanned something in another app? Open USDZ, STL, OBJ or PLY files.

CLEAN
Remove stray bits of table, smooth out scan noise without shrinking the part, flatten the base, and lay it flat on the bed with one tap.

SIZE
Type the exact width, depth or height. Calibrate against a caliper measurement so the whole part matches the real thing. Add fit tolerance for parts that slide into something, or mirror for the opposite hand.

REBUILD (Pro)
Truefit detects round and block-shaped parts and replaces the bumpy scan with a clean, exact solid. Edit the diameter, height, corners and chamfers, and add a round, D-shaft or hex-nut hole.

EXPORT
Save a watertight STL in millimeters, Z up, ready for Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura or any slicer. See the size, volume and estimated filament before you print.

Truefit Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly through your Apple ID. It renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel any time in Settings.

Scanning requires an iPhone with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro or a later Pro model).

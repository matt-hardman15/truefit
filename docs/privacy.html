Truefit Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026.

Truefit does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Camera and scans

Truefit uses your camera and LiDAR scanner only while you are capturing a scan. Photos and 3D models are processed and stored on your device. They are never uploaded by Truefit. Files you export go only where you choose to send them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Truefit receives only whether your subscription is active, never your payment details.

Analytics and tracking

Truefit contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project's support page.
